WHAT REALLY HAPPENED
Most mill women stayed in Lowell for a few years. Many went home, married or moved west. Some, like Lucy Larcom, Harriet Robinson and Sarah Bagley, became writers, teachers and reformers. By the 1850s, Irish immigrants made up a large share of the mill workers, often for lower pay.
